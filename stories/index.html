How it works

A public story. An independent take. An optional conversation.

Step 1

When we can, we embed the original public Reddit post with Reddit’s own tools so attribution stays with the author.

Step 2

See what stands out

Coach-Halo adds context, questions, and possible next steps. Unless we say otherwise, that analysis is ours, not the featured coach’s personal writing.

Editorial standards

Embedded Reddit posts remain on Reddit. The author is not affiliated with Coach-Halo and has not been coached by us unless we explicitly say so.

Coach-Halo analysis is an independent educational interpretation. We do not present AI-generated wording as a coach’s personal quote.

Some pages are clearly labeled composite examples. Those are not verified biographies.

Individual experiences are not guarantees. Perspectives are not a replacement for medical, mental health, legal, or financial advice.
