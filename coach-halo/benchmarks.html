Choose where you fit

  1. Consider the lower end while building experience and client results.
  2. Start near the suggested rate when your experience and service are comparable to the providers sampled.
  3. Consider the higher end when you offer specialized expertise, strong demonstrated results, or substantial additional support.

Review your pricing as you learn. Consistent bookings and limited availability may support a higher rate. If prospects hesitate, consider your audience, offer, and how clearly you explain the value.

Use this as a starting point. Your experience, location, clientele, and what’s included may justify a different price.
