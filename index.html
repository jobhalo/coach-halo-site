Frequently asked questions

What’s the difference between free and paid?

Free keeps you in the network. You may receive opportunity alerts, including ones from third-party lead platforms. Those platforms may charge you to contact a lead. Paid is hands-on support: we match your profile, follow up, and work to turn interest into a conversation. Paid enrollment is open by specialty, not for every kind of coaching.

Can every coach join the paid program?

Enrollment depends on your coaching specialty and current demand. Complete the short form to see your options. If enrollment is closed, you can join the free network and hear from us when it opens.

Do you take a commission?

No. The paid program is a flat subscription. You keep what you earn from your clients.

Who sets my price and availability?

You do. You set your own pricing, availability, and terms.
