Frequently asked questions

How much does Coach-Halo cost?

Coach-Halo is completely free. However, each partner platform has its own pricing and eligibility requirements. To qualify for automated referrals, coaches must have an active paid plan with verified Coach-Halo affiliate attribution. MentorCruise is an exception, requiring only an approved, active mentor profile.

Existing partner accounts and independent affiliate programs are subject to manual review and approval.

Am I guaranteed clients?

No. Referrals depend on the availability of relevant coaching opportunities, your coaching specialty, and fluctuations in market and seasonal demand.

What are the benefits of joining the partner network?

You can benefit from your chosen partner platform's own ecosystem, tools, and growth opportunities, while receiving additional client referrals through Coach-Halo at no extra cost.

Do I need to contact potential clients?

No. Coach-Halo handles the initial outreach and directs matching leads to your coaching profile or website.

How does Coach-Halo make money?

Coach-Halo may earn commissions through its affiliate partnerships, allowing us to offer our referral service at no additional cost to coaches.

Can I join with my own affiliate program?

Yes. If you operate your own coaching business and offer an affiliate program, you can apply to partner directly with Coach-Halo. All applications are subject to manual review.
